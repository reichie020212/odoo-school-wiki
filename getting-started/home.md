# Getting Started

Step-by-step from "I just cloned the repo" to "I'm logged in and poking around".

1. **[What is the School Management System](01-overview.md)** — high-level overview: what modules exist, who uses them, what the homepage and portal look like.
2. **[Installing the stack](02-installation.md)** — Docker Compose, creating a database, installing the full suite with demo data.
3. **[First login and initial setup](03-first-login.md)** — log in, set your school name and logo, grant School Manager rights, tour the top menu.
4. **[Demo users](04-demo-users.md)** — ready-to-use accounts for the student, guardian, teacher, and registrar roles seeded by the demo data.

Once you're comfortable, jump to the [Admin Guides](../admin-guides/home) for everyday workflows.
